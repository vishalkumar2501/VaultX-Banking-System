2. Retail Lending & Loan Products (Interest Rates & Processing Fees)

VaultX provides retail loan products linked to the RBI External Benchmark Lending Rate (EBLR):

  • VaultX Home Loan: Starting interest rate of 8.35% per annum for credit score ≥ 750. Maximum repayment tenure is 30 years. Processing fee is 0.25% of loan amount (capped at a maximum of ₹10,000). Zero prepayment penalty on floating rate loans.
  • VaultX Personal Loan: Unsecured personal loans available from ₹50,000 up to ₹25,00,000. Interest rates range from 10.25% to 14.50% per annum based on employer tier and salary. Tenure options: 12 months to 60 months.
  • VaultX Green EV Auto Loan: Special subsidized rate of 8.65% per annum for Electric Vehicles with 100% on-road financing for selected models. Regular diesel/petrol vehicle rate is 9.15% per annum.
  • VaultX Global Education Loan: Interest rate of 8.90% per annum for premier domestic and international universities with a flexible moratorium period of Course Duration + 12 months.

4. Digital Payments, Daily Limits & Charges

  • UPI Daily Transfer Limit: ₹1,00,000 per calendar day or maximum 20 transactions per bank account (as regulated by NPCI). Peer-to-peer (P2P) transfers are 100% free of charge.
  • ATM Cash Withdrawal: ₹50,000 per day for Gold debit card; ₹1,00,000 per day for Platinum debit card. Cash withdrawals abroad incur a flat ₹125 + 3.5% forex markup fee.
  • IMPS Daily Limit: ₹5,00,000 per day with a nominal fee of ₹5 for transfers between ₹1,00,000 and ₹5,00,000.
  • NEFT & RTGS: Completely free 24x7x365 through VaultX Net Banking and Mobile App.

5. Security, Fraud Liability & Grievance Redressal Policy

Under the RBI Zero Liability Charter:

  • Zero Liability Window: If a customer reports an unauthorized electronic debit within 3 working days of receiving the SMS/email alert, the customer has ZERO liability. The bank must reverse the debited amount within 10 working days.
  • Delayed Reporting (4 to 7 Working Days): Customer liability is capped at a maximum of ₹10,000 for regular savings accounts and ₹25,000 for current accounts.
  • Reporting Beyond 7 Days: Liability is subject to bank board-approved investigation policy.
